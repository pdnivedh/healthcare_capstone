
HOSPITAL ANALYTICS SEMANTIC LAYER
=================================

DATA SOURCE
- Synthea synthetic hospital records.
- Analysis cohort: 2017–2025.
- Prototype only. Not for clinical use.

ADMISSION
- One row in syn_stays represents one inpatient stay.
- Total admissions: COUNT(*) FROM syn_stays.

PATIENT
- Patient identifier: PATIENT.
- Count unique patients using COUNT(DISTINCT PATIENT).

30-DAY READMISSION
- Column: syn_stays.readmit_30d.
- TRUE indicates a new inpatient admission within 30 days
  after discharge from the index stay.
- Count readmitted stays using:
  WHERE readmit_30d = TRUE

LENGTH OF STAY (LOS)
- Column: syn_stays.los_days.
- Unit: days.
- Average LOS: AVG(los_days).

AGE
- Column: syn_stays.age.
- Unit: years.
- Age greater than 65: age > 65.

SEX / GENDER
- Column: syn_stays.GENDER.
- Values: M and F.

RACE
- Column: syn_stays.RACE.

READMISSION RISK
- Table: syn_risk_scores.
- Column: risk_lr.
- Predicted 30-day readmission probability, 0 to 1.
- Prefer risk_lr for risk-based analysis.

HIGH RISK
- Defined as the top 10% of admissions ranked by risk_lr.
- Do not interpret this as a clinical risk threshold.

LACE SCORE
- Column: syn_risk_scores.lace.
- Readmission risk benchmark.

YEAR
- Extract the calendar year from syn_stays.START.
- Example: EXTRACT(YEAR FROM START).

IMPORTANT RULES
- Use only the approved hospital tables.
- Never invent table or column names.
- syn_risk_scores does not contain adm_id or reason.
- Do not join syn_stays and syn_risk_scores using PATIENT alone,
  because one patient can have multiple admissions.
- Only generate read-only analytical SQL.
